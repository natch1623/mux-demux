## Prompt del portal

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one finished standalone circular fantasy portal medallion game UI button on genuinely transparent background, square canvas. It represents a DEMUX gateway into four fictional worlds: Adventure Time Marceline, Genshin Snezhnaya, Marvel Venom, JoJo DIO. NO characters, NO faces, NO logos, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO interface mockup. A coherent finely crafted round astrolabe portal with bright icy diamond-shaped core and four elegantly interwoven materials around it: wine red string-like strokes and bat-wing carving at top, sapphire crystal and silver frost at right, glossy obsidian symbiote tendrils and white curved highlights below, gold clockwork and a tiny emerald heart at left. Integrated continuous circular frame, not four separate buttons, not a pie chart, no visible sector dividers. Cool pale silver outer rim with restrained antique gold inlay, deep midnight blue interior, sharp detailed engraving, strong restrained central light, premium anime game relic illustration, sculpted relief and readable silhouette. At small 120px diameter must read as a beautiful unified portal, not a noisy color wheel. Perfect circle fills 94% of canvas, even transparent outer padding and corners, no outside glow haze, no shadows cast on background.
